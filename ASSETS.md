# Assets

The MARC logo and IIM Trichy logo in `dist/assets/` are unchanged copies of the supplied logo files.

The white and maroon polo images are transparent cutouts derived from the supplied campaign posters using image generation background extraction. The extraction prompts requested removal of poster backgrounds, lettering and surrounding shirts while preserving the polo, fabric and embroidered crest. The white image also received an edge-cleanup pass. Review embroidery and edge fidelity against the original artwork before treating an edited cutout as an exact manufacturing reference.

Final repository assets:

- `dist/assets/marc-logo.png`
- `dist/assets/iim-trichy-logo.png`
- `dist/assets/polo-white.png`
- `dist/assets/polo-maroon.png`
